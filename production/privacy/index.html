Investor Survey privacy

Property Kickstart is responsible for information collected through this survey. For privacy enquiries or to exercise your rights, email privacy@PropertyKickstart.co.uk.

You can complete the survey without providing your name, email address or mobile number. We use survey answers, the survey year, a submission reference and submission time to understand investor priorities and improve our services. Where you provide an email address, we may link your answers to an existing or future investor profile or assessment.

Completing the survey does not subscribe you to marketing. Marketing email consent is a separate, optional choice. Choosing the WhatsApp invitation only displays a joining link and does not automatically enrol you. We do not publish individually identifiable survey responses.

We use appropriate service providers to securely host the survey, process submissions and manage records. We retain personal information only as long as necessary for its purposes and then delete or anonymise it as appropriate.

For our full explanation of lawful bases, recipients, international transfers, retention and your data-protection rights, read the Property Kickstart Privacy Notice.
