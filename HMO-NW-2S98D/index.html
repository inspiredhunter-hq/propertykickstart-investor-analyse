6. What could change these figures?

What happens if the income or costs change after you buy? The figures above are estimates based on today's assumptions. Before making a decision, it is worth thinking about three things:

1. Rental incomeA room might be empty or tenants may change, reducing the rent received.
2. Running costsRepairs, insurance, bills or licensing costs may be higher than expected.
3. Mortgage interestIf you borrow, interest rates and refinancing terms can change your monthly costs.

These are things to investigate, not reasons automatically to reject a property. Detailed scenario calculations are deliberately outside this introductory assessment.
