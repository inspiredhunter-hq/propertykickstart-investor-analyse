7. Stress-test the numbers

What could change these figures?

What happens if the income or costs change after you buy? The figures above are estimates based on today's assumptions. Before making a decision, consider three things:

1. Rental incomeTenants may leave or rent might not be received for a period.
2. Running costsRepairs, insurance or other property expenses may be higher than expected.
3. Mortgage interestIf you borrow, changing interest rates or refinancing terms may increase your monthly costs.

These are factors to investigate, not automatic reasons to reject a property.
