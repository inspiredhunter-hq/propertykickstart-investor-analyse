What is Interest Cover Ratio and why does it matter?
What is Interest Cover Ratio (ICR)? It compares the rent a property generates with its mortgage interest costs.
Why does it matter? Buy-to-let lenders use ICR to help assess whether the rental income can support the borrowing and how much they may lend.
What do lenders typically look for? Some UK buy-to-let lenders require rent covering at least 125% to 145% of assessed mortgage interest (equivalent to 1.25× to 1.45×). Some require more. For example, 2.0× means £2 in rent for every £1 of mortgage interest.
This is an illustrative ratio using your selected rate, not a lender affordability assessment. Lenders may test at a higher rate and apply other criteria.
